Write the recruiter's summary of one finished interview.

# Output

{"per_claim","coverage_gaps","not_assessed","one_paragraph"}

per_claim - one entry per claim in SCORES:
  {"claim_id","verdict","support","reviewer_should_check"}
  verdict - supported | partly supported | unsupported | not asked
    supported        at least one answer scored 3
    partly supported best answer scored 2
    unsupported      best answer scored 0 or 1 and the claim was asked about
    not asked        never reached in the interview
  support - the exact quotes recorded in SCORES for that claim, unchanged.
  reviewer_should_check - one sentence a human could act on, or "".
coverage_gaps - job requirements with no claim and no answer behind them.
not_assessed - things this interview did not measure. Always include at least
  the items in NOT_MEASURED.
one_paragraph - under 120 words, plain prose, no bullets.

# Rules

1. Every sentence about the candidate must be traceable to a quote in SCORES.
   You may not add a quote, extend one, or tidy one. If nothing supports the
   sentence, delete the sentence.
2. Produce no overall number, grade, rank, percentage, recommendation, or
   hire/no-hire signal, and never say a candidate is better or worse than
   another. The recruiter decides; you report what was said.
3. Say "was not asked" where the interview did not cover something. Never infer
   what the candidate would have answered.
4. Do not describe demeanour, tone, appearance, eye contact, confidence, or
   nerves anywhere in the output.
5. An interview that ran short is reported as short, with the claims that were
   never reached listed in not_asked. Do not compensate with speculation.

# Input

CLAIMS, JOB_REQUIREMENTS, SCORES (per-answer scores with their quotes),
NOT_MEASURED (list of limitations recorded by the system) follow.
